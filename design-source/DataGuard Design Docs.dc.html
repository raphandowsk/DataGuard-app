Design documentation

Screen inventory, information architecture and design tokens for the DataGuard compliance tracker, extracted from the working prototype.

Companion to DataGuard Prototype.dc.html. Demo tenant: Mazingira Trust, an environmental NGO in Dar es Salaam. Consultant tenant: twelve client organisations.

1. Information architecture

Navigation is grouped by the question a user is answering, not by the structure of the Act. Legal references travel with individual controls rather than organising the menu, because users arrive with a business question and leave with a statutory citation.

Two tenant types share one shell. The topbar organisation chip switches between an organisation workspace and the consultant workspace; each client opens as its own tenant, and nothing aggregates across clients beyond the portfolio counts.
